About

Second Hug Plush Co. restores beloved plush toys through cleaning, repair, and reconstruction. Every plush has a story; we help preserve it.

Second Hug Plush Co. was born out of a personal love for all things plush. We have always found comfort in our fluffy friends of all shapes and sizes and have seen them as not just objects, but an extension of our family. As we get older, our friends age with us and we believe they deserve to be pampered too! Second Hug Plush Co. restores beloved plush toys through cleaning, repair, and reconstruction. We believe worn companions deserve a second life.
