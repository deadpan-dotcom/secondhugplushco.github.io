Basic Refresh

A gentle refresh for lightly worn plush toys that need surface-level care.

Includes soft cleaning, deodorizing, light reshaping, and surface dust removal. Ideal for plushes that are loved but structurally intact.

Standard Restoration

A deeper restoration service for plush toys showing moderate wear or aging.

Includes stitch repair, seam reinforcement, restuffing where needed, and careful surface cleaning to restore softness and shape.

Advanced Restoration

For heavily worn, damaged, or partially deconstructed plush toys requiring structural work.

Includes fabric patching, internal reconstruction, limb or seam rebuilding, and stabilization of weakened areas to extend long-term durability.

Heirloom Care

A preservation-focused service for sentimental or fragile plush companions.

Designed for items that may not be used regularly but are deeply meaningful. Focuses on stabilization, gentle restoration, and long-term preservation techniques.
